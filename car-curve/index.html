About professional CAP HPI data ↗
How the curve is calculated

Published-reference mode: the new list price and 3-year/36,000-mile resale forecast are the only published values. Years between them use constant-percentage interpolation: value = list price × (3-year resale ÷ list price)^(age ÷ 3). After year 3, the selected annual depreciation assumption applies. The curve is not observed historical pricing.

Different registration year or unlisted derivative: no benchmark is automatically transferred. You must opt into a generic scenario and enter a price. RAC reports broad new-car losses of 15–35% in year 1 and 40–60% across three years. Our default generic path uses the midpoints: 25% lost at year 1 and 50% at year 3, with geometric interpolation. This is not a RAC forecast for your vehicle. Some cars fall outside those ranges.

Value today: starts at the price you supply in the current calendar year, with a constant annual percentage loss you choose. No previous price history is inferred and no published new-car benchmark is used.

Changing the new price: in published mode, the three-year retained fraction is held constant and scaled to your entered price. That makes it a custom scenario, not the source’s cash forecast. Source markers are shown only for the unmodified original-price reference.

What’s not modelled: individual mileage, condition, options, powertrain changes, finance interest, maintenance, inflation and future market shocks. Reference forecasts assume 36,000 miles at year 3. No invented mileage adjustment or statistical confidence interval is applied. All amounts are nominal GBP; “per month” is depreciation only, not a finance payment. References describe cars offered when the article was written, not every vehicle registered in that year.

Source quality: prices and three-year resale amounts are transcribed from the forecast rows, not the live advertising widgets. More recent duplicate references take priority. Inconsistent MINI Countryman arithmetic and ambiguous Volkswagen Tayron / Vauxhall Grandland derivative descriptions were excluded. Where noted, derived totals are recomputed from the two published prices.

Live coverage: CAP HPI documents authenticated derivative/current/future valuation APIs; they require suitable commercial access and display rights. No CAP API subscription is connected. Honest John’s former valuation pages now redirect to Drive, so they were not used. There is no unattended data refresh. References checked 20 October 2026.
